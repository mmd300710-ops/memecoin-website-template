FREQUENTLY ASKED

What is DarkOx Finance?
DarkOx Finance in this template is a fictional DeFi-style landing page concept. Connect it to your real project information before presenting it as a live service.
Can I connect a wallet?
No wallet connection is implemented in this file. A secure wallet connection requires additional integration and careful transaction validation.
Are the tokenomics and dashboard numbers real?
No. All example figures are placeholders for the visual design. Replace them with verified information and a reliable data source.
Do the Farms buttons deposit tokens?
No. They display demo notifications only. Real staking or farming needs audited smart contracts, wallet integration, and a secure transaction flow.
Does the website play sound or vibrate?
The sound control uses browser-generated ambient audio after user interaction. Vibration is attempted only on supported devices and browsers.
